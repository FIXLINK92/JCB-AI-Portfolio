## Why the distinction matters

I use AI tools aggressively to accelerate delivery, but I separate tool familiarity from evidence-backed capability. If a role requires a tool I have not used deeply, I prefer to demonstrate how quickly I can learn it through a scoped working task rather than claim experience I cannot defend in an interview.
